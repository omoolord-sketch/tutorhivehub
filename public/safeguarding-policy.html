Safeguarding Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

TutorHiveHub is committed to providing a safe, respectful, and professional online learning environment for students.

Student Safety

Lessons should be delivered in a structured online environment with respectful communication and appropriate boundaries.

Tutor Review

Tutors are reviewed for subject knowledge, communication, professionalism, reliability, and suitability for online academic support.

Parent Communication

Parents should be kept informed about student support, progress, concerns, and next steps where appropriate.

Reporting Concerns

Safeguarding or wellbeing concerns should be reported promptly to TutorHiveHub at info@tutorhivehub.com.
